Histora brings world history to life through interactive stories, engaging quizzes, and fascinating characters from ancient civilizations to modern times. ✨ Features: • Explore civilizations across all continents and eras • Learn about influential historical figures worldwide • Interactive quizzes to test your knowledge • Track your learning progress and achievements • Beautiful, intuitive interface for all ages 📚 Topics Include: Ancient Egypt, Rome, Greece • Medieval kingdoms and empires • Renaissance and Enlightenment • Industrial Revolution • Modern history and beyond Perfect for students, history enthusiasts, and lifelong learners who want to understand the world's past.

  4. Descriptions: Copy the short and full descriptions into "Store listing" section
  5. Colors: Primary: #163D31 | Accent: #D6A84F | All assets use consistent branding
  6. Scope: All assets emphasize GLOBAL history (not region-specific) with English language positioning
