Assumptions to check

  • Screens are rebuilt, not screenshots. Layouts follow the store captures (era route, reader, quiz sheet, chapter route, unlock window, Home) in today's orange accent. Text and images come from the app itself. The tab bar shows Home, Explore and Profile as in 1.4.5.
  • The quiz is the real chapter-1 question from scripts/seed/quizvragen/julius-caesar.json: “With whom did Caesar form the First Triumvirate?” Answer B, Pompey and Crassus, with the app's own explanation. The reader shows the real opening of chapter 1 and its caption.
  • The era route is simplified to Alexander → Caesar → Cleopatra. The real Antiquity route has more stops in between (Ashoka, Spartacus). Lifespans shown: Alexander 356–323 BC, Caesar 100–44 BC, Cleopatra 69–30 BC.
  • “A few chapters later…” stands in for reading the rest of the story, so no chapter count appears anywhere. The reader header says “Chapter 1” without “of 8”.
  • Example values: a streak going from 6 to 7 days, 290 Ducats on Home. +10 Ducats per finished chapter is the app's real rate.
  • The fox appears only as the app's own art (waving, pointing, cheering, travelling, the laurel-wreath era head). It is never redrawn and never named.
  • “Every chapter opens with a painting” is the same claim as the store listing, which was checked against the content.
  • Not in this cut: trivia, the shop and the widget. The Home screen at the end does show the “Test your history skills!” card.
