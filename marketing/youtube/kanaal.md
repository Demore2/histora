## Beschrijving (plakken onder Aanpassen → Basisinfo)

What if you lived the life of Julius Caesar? Napoleon? Khalid ibn al-Walid?

Histora tells the real lives of history's greatest figures as if they were yours: where you're born, who you love, the battles you win, the mistakes you make, and how it all ends. Short "what if" videos and longer stories, all animated in 3D.

Every story is checked against historical sources. Where historians disagree, we say so.

Who should be next? Tell us in the comments.
