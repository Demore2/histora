When Napoleon died on St Helena in 1821, his doctor measured him at 5 pieds 2 pouces: five foot two, but in old French units. The short, furious Napoleon was a British invention: cartoonists like James Gillray drew him as "Little Boney", a tiny man in a permanent rage.
