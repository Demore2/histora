### Voice-over (clean, for TTS)
Napoleon wasn't short. So who made him small? When he died, he was measured at five foot two. But those were French inches, and they were longer. In English measures, he was about five foot six. At least as tall as the average Frenchman of his day. British cartoonists drew him as Little Boney, a tiny man in a rage. His soldiers did call him the little corporal, but out of affection. He conquered an empire. And lost to a cartoon. That's thirty seconds of history.

## Fact notes
- **Measured at 5 ft 2 at his death** — his doctor Francesco Antommarchi's autopsy on St Helena (May 1821) gives 5 *pieds* 2 *pouces* (4 *lignes*), in the old French units.
- **French inches were longer** — a *pouce* was about 2.707 cm, an English inch 2.54 cm. 5 pieds 2 pouces ≈ 1.68-1.69 m ≈ 5 ft 6 in — standard.
- **"At least as tall as the average Frenchman"** — French conscripts of his generation averaged roughly 1.64-1.65 m (anthropometric studies of conscription records, e.g. Komlos). Historians put him at average or slightly above; the video says "at least as tall", not "tall".
- **The myth bar (5'2" = 1.57 m)** is the French figure read as English feet and inches, which is how the myth arose.
- **"Little Boney"** — the British nickname, used by James Gillray and other caricaturists; the image is Gillray's *Maniac-Ravings — or — Little Boney in a Strong Fit*, published 24 May 1803 (Yale Center for British Art, CC0).
- **"The little corporal"** (*le petit caporal*) — the affectionate nickname his soldiers gave him during the Italian campaign of 1796, traditionally after the battle of Lodi.
- **Second cartoon** — James Gillray, *The King of Brobdingnag, and Gulliver*, 26 June 1803 (Metropolitan Museum of Art, public domain): George III, as Swift's giant king, inspects a tiny Napoleon through a spyglass. Eerst stond hier twee keer dezelfde spotprent; de eigenaar wilde een tweede beeld.
- **"He conquered an empire"** — Emperor of the French 1804-1814/15; the shot is the Egypt campaign (1798) from the app's scenes.
- **Not in the video, on purpose:** that his Imperial Guard was unusually tall (true as a requirement, but a second cause makes the hook less clean).
