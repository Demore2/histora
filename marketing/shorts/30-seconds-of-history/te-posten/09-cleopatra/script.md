### Voice-over (clean, for TTS)
Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the building of the Great Pyramid. So who was she, really? At eighteen, she became queen of Egypt. She won over Julius Caesar. Then Mark Antony. At Actium, Rome crushed their fleet. A year later, she was dead. But Cleopatra wasn't Egyptian. Her family were Macedonian Greeks, from one of Alexander the Great's generals. In almost three hundred years, she was the first of them to learn Egyptian. That's thirty seconds of history.

## Fact notes
- **Great Pyramid c. 2560 BC** (Khufu, Fourth Dynasty; dates c. 2600-2560 BC) — standard. Cleopatra died August 30 BC; Apollo 11 landed July 1969. 2560 - 30 = **2,530 years**; 30 BC → AD 1969 is **1,998 years** (there is no year 0). The claim holds for any date of the pyramid within the usual range.
- **Queen at eighteen, 51 BC** — born 69 BC; co-ruler with her brother Ptolemy XIII from 51 BC — standard (Plutarch, Cassius Dio).
- **Julius Caesar, 48 BC** — Caesar in Alexandria from 48 BC; their son Caesarion born 47 BC. The carpet scene is Plutarch's (*Caesar* 49: rolled in a bedsack/rug), hence it is only an image here.
- **Mark Antony, 41 BC** — the meeting at Tarsus, Plutarch *Antony* 26 (the golden barge with purple sails).
- **Actium, 2 September 31 BC** — Octavian's fleet under Agrippa defeated Antony and Cleopatra; she escaped with part of the fleet — Plutarch *Antony* 65-68, Cassius Dio 50.
- **Dead a year later** — August 30 BC, in Alexandria. The video doesn't mention the snake: ancient writers already said nobody knew how she died (Plutarch *Antony* 86).
- **Macedonian Greeks, from one of Alexander's generals** — the Ptolemaic dynasty descends from Ptolemy I Soter, Alexander's Macedonian general, who took Egypt after 323 BC (king from 305 BC). Her mother is not known for certain; the line the video describes is the paternal one.
- **"In almost three hundred years, the first of them to learn Egyptian"** — Plutarch, *Antony* 27: she spoke many languages, "whereas the kings before her had not even taken the trouble to learn the Egyptian language". Ptolemaic rule 323/305-30 BC ≈ 275-290 years, hence "almost three hundred".
- **Map** — Pella (Macedon) to Alexandria, a straight line; it marks the family's origin, not a journey she made.
