On 18 July 1969, two days before the landing, speechwriter William Safire sent the White House a memo titled "In Event of Moon Disaster". It began: "Fate has ordained that the men who went to the moon to explore in peace will stay on the moon to rest in peace." The lunar module's ascent engine had no backup: if it failed to fire, Armstrong and Aldrin could not leave. After the moonwalk, Aldrin noticed that the circuit breaker that armed that engine had snapped off. He pushed it in with a felt-tip pen, and the engine fired. The speech was never needed.
