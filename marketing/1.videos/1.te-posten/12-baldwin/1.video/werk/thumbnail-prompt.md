# Thumbnail Baldwin IV — nieuwe stijl (naar de referenties in marketing/referenties/thumbnails/)

Getekend portret, geen blokpoppetjes. Plak de prompt in Higgsfield (gpt_image_2_5, 16:9, high; vraagt een Basic-plan)
of in ChatGPT, en zet het resultaat neer als `werk/thumb-bron.png`. Daarna zet Claude de tekst erop
("BALDWIN IV" + "1161 – 1185" / "THE LEPER KING"), in de vorm van de referenties: naam groot rechts, jaartallen eronder.

## Prompt

YouTube history thumbnail illustration in bold graphic-novel style: clean thick ink outlines, rich flat colours with
soft shading, like a modern illustrated history channel. On the LEFT THIRD, a half-body portrait of a young medieval
king of Jerusalem, about 16 years old, pale face with a few faint reddish lesions on his cheek, fierce determined eyes
looking at the viewer, short light-brown hair, a simple gold crown, white tunic and a royal blue cloak, both hands
wrapped in white linen bandages resting on the hilt of a sword. Behind him, warm golden-hour sky, the stone walls and
towers of medieval Jerusalem with blue banners, and far in the distance a huge desert army with golden banners. The
RIGHT HALF of the image is calm open sky and wall, left empty for large title text. No text, no letters, no crosses,
no symbols on clothing.

## Variant met masker (herkenning uit Kingdom of Heaven)

Zelfde prompt, maar: "his face hidden behind a smooth silver mask with dark eye slits, a white linen hood under the crown".
